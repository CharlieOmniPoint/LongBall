# Player replies for past games

The owner's 24 October clarification makes historic games read-only for player availability. Existing replies, selection, attendance and match history remain visible.

A new player reply is offered and accepted only for a scheduled game dated today or later in Europe/London, with no accepted evidence that it has started, completed or been abandoned. Passing the planned kickoff alone does not end an unplayed game. Invalid dates fail closed. Cancelled/postponed games cannot collect replies.

The shared rule is used by the local native/browser player view, the connected schedule and both SQLite/PostgreSQL own-player command paths. The player home already excludes past and recorded active/completed games. Existing command receipts can still replay without changing historical data. Managers retain their authorised historical correction controls. Training behaviour is unchanged by this game-specific clarification.

Verification covers London midnight and both daylight-saving transitions; today/future incomplete summaries; past/active/completed denials with unchanged saved state; receipt replay after closure; manager corrections; and Player Schedule cards at both phone widths. Source-bound results are recorded after the accepted build. No migration or new service is required. Existing physical-device, privacy/support and dependency release gates remain unchanged.
