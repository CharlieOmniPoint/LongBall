# Results and private reporting implementation

The fictional local application now has serializable historical results, corrected-result audit, archived detailed matches, season 3/1/0 points and chronological form, match and season participation, position/preference coverage and separate game/training attendance evidence. The supplied 3–1 / 2–2 / 0–1 example produces P3/W1/D1/L1/GF5/GA4/GD+1/Pts4. Ordinary friendlies count; unknown scores, abandoned games and exceptional outcomes do not silently receive ordinary points.

Historical final scores are summary evidence. A 3–1 game with one credited Roma goal retains two unassigned Roma goals; no clock, minutes, selection, attendance, goal type/time, award window or old notification is invented. Corrections keep before/after values, reasons, revisions and idempotent command identities. Known kick-offs use absolute instants; date-only games with ambiguous same-day ordering do not manufacture form order.

Detailed participation derives from observed integer-millisecond intervals at the latest recorded checkpoint. The match snapshot preserves position preferences; unknown primary goalkeeper evidence remains separate. Zero-minute attendees count as attended and do not count as played appearances. Position totals conserve playing time. Match targets use the existing Q07 experimental availability-interval calculation, exact band comparisons and an explicit sole-keeper policy; the screen labels this experiment. Managers can audit a target-exception reason on an archived record without changing its minutes or result.

Attendance separates actual records from responses and expected/selected status. Cancelled sessions, non-expected players and explicit non-selection are excluded. Unknown historical squads remain coverage gaps, not invented eligible attendance. Rates expose present/known-record and recorded/eligible numerators and denominators; empty denominators are not applicable. Q09 denominator and excused handling remain **proposed**, visibly labelled, with excused counts separate.

Players receive only their explicit player's named participation, intervals and attendance; spectator report shapes omit those fields. Unconfirmed competition policy denies shared scores, goals, points, form and scorer aggregates. One permitted result does not reveal aggregates containing withheld results. Fixture reclassification invalidates prior sharing. Policy/classification versions and evidence/generation times travel with projections.

The connected service now implements authenticated report/export routes, role-filtered projections and current identity/scope checks, covered by API/access and browser tests. Competition-sharing decisions remain service-owned; unapproved policy withholds shared results. Published photos and final awards live in the separate community module; this record store reports them as not recorded rather than inventing or resetting them. The native journal and physical-device recovery behaviour require their separate native verification gates.

Validation at handoff: 21 scoped records tests passed; root and mobile TypeScript passed. Tests cover unknown history, 3/1/0 correction, live goal correction followed by re-archive, policy suppression/reclassification, player/spectator filtering, explicit non-selection/unknown/excused attendance, zero-minute attendance, interval/position conservation, primary-preference history, UTC ordering, metadata, exact targets and exception audit. Browser journeys are tracked separately in `full-build-plan.md`.
